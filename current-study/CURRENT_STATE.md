# Current State in Plain Language

Updated: 5 September 2026

## The short answer

The active thesis is now about **why Thai IT students would pursue short online IT professional certificates available through platforms such as Coursera**. It is no longer limited to university-issued digital badges used in job applications.

The study uses the Theory of Planned Behavior. In simple terms, it tests whether students are more likely to intend to pursue a certificate when:

- they think pursuing it is a good, positive, and helpful idea;
- people whose opinions matter to them think they should pursue it; and
- they believe they have the ability, knowledge, skills, money, and resources to pursue it.

## What is borrowed directly

Hunsinger and Smith (2008) studied information-systems undergraduates deciding whether to pursue IT certification. The current thesis uses their:

- published framework;
- three TPB variables leading to Behavioral Intention;
- three tested and supported hypotheses;
- printed questionnaire wording and scoring procedure;
- referent groups and control factors; and
- 12-month time horizon.

Wang (2023) independently tested and supported the same three paths among 525 undergraduates considering MOOCs. The thesis includes direct crops of both published framework diagrams before presenting the framework constructed for this study.

The questionnaire changes only the name of the target from `IT certification` to `short online IT professional certificate`. It does not invent a new scale or silently convert the source items to a different Likert scale.

## Current study design

- Population: students aged 18 or older, currently enrolled as undergraduates in IT-related programmes at universities in Thailand.
- Credential: an assessed online IT programme offered by a company or university through Coursera, edX, Udacity, or a similar platform, shorter than a degree, with a certificate after successful completion.
- Independent variables: Attitude, Subjective Norm, and Perceived Behavioral Control.
- Dependent variable: Behavioral Intention to pursue the certificate within the next 12 months.
- Questionnaire: 26 source-based measurement responses using the published seven-point formats.
- Pilot: 40 eligible students, excluded from the main sample.
- Main recruitment target: 400 responses, retaining at least 384 eligible and complete responses.
- Main analysis: one multiple linear regression for H1-H3.
- Language: English.
- No interviews and no experimental scenarios.
