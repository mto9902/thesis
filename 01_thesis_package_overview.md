## Core research logic

This thesis is not asking whether students simply "accept" micro-credentials.

It asks:

**Which visible features make Thai IT students see an IT micro-credential as credible and valuable?**

The main logic is:

- students cannot directly observe the true market value of a micro-credential
- they rely on signals such as recognition, stackability, and industry endorsement
- those signals shape trust
- trust shapes perceived value
