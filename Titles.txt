Between those two, the stronger thesis title is:

Stackability, Recognition, and Student Trust in IT Micro-Credentials in Thailand

It sounds more researchable, more distinctive, and more policy-relevant. “Stackability” is especially useful because it gives the topic a stronger edge than a generic trust/value study.

The second one is good too:

Recognition and Trust in IT Micro-Credentials in Thailand: What Makes Students See Them as Valuable?

This one is more readable and more natural in plain English, but slightly less sharp academically.

My recommendation:

Use the first as the thesis title.
Use the second as the plain-language explanation of the study.
