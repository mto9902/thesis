# Scope and Terms

## Working title

**Factors Influencing Thai IT Students' Intentions to Pursue Short Online IT Professional Certificates**

An optional subtitle is: **A Theory of Planned Behavior Study**.

## Main research question

What factors influence Thai university students in IT-related programmes to intend to pursue a short online IT professional certificate within the next 12 months?

## Target credential

For this study, a **short online IT professional certificate** means a credential that:

- is earned by completing one or more online IT-related courses;
- requires an assessment, project, examination, or other demonstration of learning;
- is issued by an industry provider or university;
- is delivered through a platform such as Coursera, edX, Udacity, or a similar service;
- takes substantially less time than a full degree; and
- provides a certificate or other shareable record of completion.

Examples include certificate programmes from Google, IBM, Microsoft, Meta, and universities. The platform delivers the learning, while the company or university is normally the credential provider.

## Standard survey description

All respondents should see one neutral description before answering the measurement questions:

> In this questionnaire, a short online IT professional certificate means an assessed online programme in an IT-related field that is offered by a company or university through a platform such as Coursera, edX, Udacity, or a similar service. It is shorter than a degree and provides a certificate after successful completion. Please answer with this type of certificate in mind.

No particular brand should be presented as better than another. Examples clarify the category; they are not experimental treatments.

## Included population

- Adults currently enrolled in an undergraduate IT-related programme at a university in Thailand
- Students with or without prior experience of online certificates

Restricting the study to undergraduates gives the closest contextual replication of Hunsinger and Smith (2008). Expanding the population to postgraduate students would be a separate design decision.

## Included fields

IT-related programmes may include information technology, information systems, computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, data science, and closely related programmes.

## Excluded credentials

- Full bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degrees
- Attendance-only badges with no assessment
- Internal course badges that have no separate certificate
- Government licences or regulated professional licences
- In-person-only training

Traditional exam-only vendor certifications, such as CCNA or CompTIA, are related but are not the focal credential type. This distinction must be stated because the closest IT-certification source studied those older forms of certification.

## Measured behavior

The study measures **behavioral intention to pursue a certificate within the next 12 months**. It does not measure actual completion, employment, salary, employer recognition, or learning outcomes.

The word "use" is too vague for the thesis because it can mean browsing a platform, starting a course, completing a course, or displaying a certificate. "Pursue" gives the questionnaire one defined behavior.
